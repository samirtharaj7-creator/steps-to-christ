Find Your Way Home

A thirteen-step journey based on Ellen G. White’s Steps to Christ

One of the most-loved little books ever written, a friendly, honest map for anyone who wants to actually know God. Thirteen short steps, in language that meets you where you are.

About this edition

Faithful to the original, written for today

This site follows the thirteen chapters and teaching of Ellen G. White's public-domain 1892 Steps to Christ.

Modern display titles, illustrations, explanations, questions, and prayers help younger readers understand and apply each chapter. Ellen White quotations retain the original wording, and Scripture quotations use the King James Version.
