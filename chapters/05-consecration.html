Step 5 of 13Consecration

All In

God does not ask for a carefully managed corner of your life. He asks for the whole heart because He wants to set the whole person free.

Step Five · All In

"And ye shall seek me, and find me, when ye shall search for me with all your heart."

Jeremiah 29:13
Start Here

There's a world of difference between giving someone a guest password and giving them the actual admin login. Guest access lets them see the harmless surface stuff. Admin means the whole account, the settings, the history, the folders you'd rather nobody opened.

Most of us try to give God guest access. He can have church on the weekend and the clean, presentable parts of our lives. The private tabs stay private.

Consecration is handing over admin. Not read-only, not a free trial you can quietly cancel, the whole account, all of it.

What This Chapter Is Saying

Half doesn't work

"The whole heart must be yielded to God." That sounds intense, and it is, but there's a reason. You can't be transformed by Someone you're only half-trusting. "We cannot be half the Lord's and half the world's. We are not God's children unless we are such entirely." It's not that God is greedy; it's that a divided heart can't actually be healed.

The hardest battle you'll fight

The book is honest that this is not easy: "The warfare against self is the greatest battle that was ever fought." Handing over the parts of your life you've been protecting feels like losing. Every instinct says hold on. But surrender here isn't defeat. It's the only door to freedom.

God does not require us to give up anything that it is for our best interest to retain. In all that He does, He has the well-being of His children in view.

Steps to Christ, chapter 5

God isn't trying to rob you

The fear underneath a lot of this is: "If I give God everything, He'll take away everything I enjoy." Read this slowly: "God does not require us to give up anything that it is for our best interest to retain." He isn't a killjoy confiscating the good stuff. He has "something vastly better to offer" than what you're chasing. What you actually surrender is "a sin-polluted heart, for Jesus to purify", and in return you get life.

The warfare against self is the greatest battle that was ever fought.

Steps to Christ, chapter 5

It comes down to your will

Maybe you're thinking, "But I've promised before and blown it. My promises are like ropes of sand." True. Here's the key the book hands you: you can't change your own heart, but you can choose. "You can give Him your will; He will then work in you to will and to do." The one thing that's truly yours to give is your yes. Give that, and God supplies the power.

Another way to see it

It sounds terrifying until you notice who's receiving it. You'd never hand a stranger your passwords, but you'd toss your car keys to a paramedic without a second thought. Surrender isn't scary in proportion to how much you give up; it's safe in proportion to how trustworthy the one you're giving it to is.

Or picture

A little kid gripping a fistful of cheap plastic beads so tightly the hand can't open, while a parent stands there offering to trade them for something real. God isn't trying to rob the fist. He's trying to fill it. But you can't receive the better thing until you let go of the lesser one.

Put It Into Practice

Who's holding the wheel?

Fear of missing out runs deep. We assume that fully following God means a boring, restricted life, that everyone else gets the good stuff while we sit out. So we hand God the "religious" parts and keep our grip on everything else.

Consecration flips the fear. It's not throwing your life away; it's handing the steering wheel to Someone who loves you completely and can see the whole road you can't. And it's not a one-time heroic moment you have to feel. It's a decision of the will you can make again every morning, even when your emotions haven't caught up. Give Him your yes; He'll handle the rest.

Try this today

Name the part of your life you keep guarding from God. Open your hands as you pray and offer that area to Him.

Think It Through

Three questions

  1. What's the part of your life you're most reluctant to hand over? What's the fear underneath it?
  2. Do you secretly believe God wants to make your life smaller? Where did that idea come from?
  3. Your feelings come and go, but your will is yours to give. Will you say yes to God today?
A Prayer

Lord, I'm scared to give You everything, but I'm more scared of running my own life. I can't change my heart, but I can choose, so here is my choice: I give You my will, all of it, today. Work in me what I can't work in myself. I trust that You're good.

Amen
